# Feature specification: Preview and handoff

Status: Proposed; blocked | Feature #45 | PBI #76 | M7.4

## User story US1 (P0)
As an author, I can inspect issues, acknowledge transformations and hand off title, body and media to LinkedIn myself.

## Acceptance scenarios
- Given converted output with warnings, when I review and acknowledge them, then I can see location, reason and recovery and choose safe options before copying.
- Given a clipboard failure or missing media, when I hand off, then failure is explicit and tested recovery guidance is provided; user retains publication control.

## Requirements and success criteria
FR-05–09; NFR-04; AC-04–06,09. See [PRD](../../docs/product/product-requirements.md) and [acceptance criteria](../../docs/testing/acceptance-criteria.md); agreed targets apply, candidate targets are not measured results.

## Scope and exclusions
Only approved LinkedIn Articles MVP; no publishing automation, mandatory upload or AI. P1 and future destinations excluded. See [MVP scope](../../docs/product/mvp-scope.md).
