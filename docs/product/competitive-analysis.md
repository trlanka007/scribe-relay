# Competitive analysis

Version 0.1 | Status: Proposed | 2026-11-24

## Positioning
ScribeRelay operates after authoring and before publication. It competes on document fidelity, destination-specific transformation and transparent accounting for content, not on writing productivity or campaign scheduling.

## Distinction from ScribeFlow
ScribeFlow focuses on writing and organizing manuscripts. ScribeRelay starts with existing content and prepares it for destination-specific transfer. Shared editor technology is not evidence of equivalent product goals. ScribeFlow could be an upstream source.

## Differentiation hypotheses
- Detect and report every unsupported or transformed source element.
- Extract and track media, with placement assistance when transfer fails.
- Separate source normalization from destination-specific rules.
- Verify output through real target-editor tests.

These are hypotheses, not claims of superiority or implemented features. Prior documentation-based observations require re-verification before public comparisons.
