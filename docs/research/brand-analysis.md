# Brand analysis

Status: Working name accepted; public clearance unresolved | 2026-11-24

ScribeRelay was selected for its intuitive association with transferring authored content without owning the writing or publishing environment.

Earlier preliminary research reported commercial usage of ScribeBridge, ScribeFlow, DraftBridge and DraftFlow, including overlapping writing or publishing products. These names were deprioritized. ScribeRelay also has a potential collision: https://scriberelay.com/ was reported to host a sign-in portal; its business purpose and overlap remain unverified.

## Decision
Use ScribeRelay internally in the private repository. Primary tagline: Write anywhere. Publish everywhere. Supporting principle: Preserve your intent.

## Open checks before public launch
- Verify current business/product usage and confusingly similar names.
- Search official trademark registries in intended markets and relevant classes.
- Verify domain registrations and availability, including .app and .dev.
- Check package namespaces and public repository discoverability.

Prior chat research is preliminary, not legal clearance; no domain purchase or trademark filing is authorized. Rebranding remains possible.
