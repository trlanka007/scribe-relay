## Implications
Use ordered traversal instead of paragraph-only extraction. Treat image occurrence identity separately from the shared asset; inventory auxiliary authored parts explicitly, or reject when reliable accounting cannot be established. These findings support the existing candidate subset, but do not establish support for complex tables, tracked changes, footnotes or every OPC relationship.

## Safety boundary
The fixture is a real DOCX package generated by python-docx with a valid PNG, not a Word-authored corpus or schema certification. `inspect()` is solely for trusted generated bytes, not uploads. It deliberately does not call the earlier tiny synthetic preflight: ordinary Office templates can exceed its experimental limits. Production preflight, complete relationship/content-type validation, resource limits, safe rejection and source-to-output reconciliation remain necessary. No production dependencies, compatibility profile changes, issue closure or LinkedIn evidence.
